Questions about lease endings

When should we start planning?

Two to three months before your lease ends. Many leases renew automatically unless you give written notice within a set window, so check your contract for the notice date.

What happens to our old copier?

It depends on your lease. Most leases end with the machine going back to the leasing company, and we can help with the return when we install your replacement.
