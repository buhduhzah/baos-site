Questions about lease endings

When should we start planning?

Two to three months before your lease ends. Many leases renew automatically unless you give written notice within a set window, so check your contract for the notice date.

What happens to our old copier?

It depends on your lease. Most leases end with the machine going back to the leasing company, and we can help with the return when we install your replacement.

Can you take over a lease early?

Sometimes. In some cases the remaining balance of your current lease can be rolled into a new one. It only makes sense when there isn't much time left, so send us your lease and we'll tell you honestly.

Our lease is with another company. Can we switch service to you now?

If your copier is a Ricoh, often yes. You keep paying your lease, we take over service, and we replace the machine when the lease ends.
