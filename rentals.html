Questions about rentals

How short can a rental be?

From about a week for an event, up to month-to-month for as long as you need it.

What's included?

Delivery, setup, and pickup when you're done. Toner and service are handled while it's with you.

What do you need from us to quote?

Your dates, the address, roughly how many pages you'll print, and whether you need color, 11x17 or stapling.

Ready for a quote?
